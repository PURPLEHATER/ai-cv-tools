ABOUT US

Volvia Interactive builds the real-time visual experiences behind some of
the most recognisable vehicles on Nordic roads. From configurators that let
a customer see their exact specification in showroom lighting, to the
displays a driver looks at every day, our work sits where design,
engineering and safety meet.

We are eighty people across Göteborg and Malmö. We are employee-owned, we
work in English, and we have shipped in this space for eleven years.

THE ROLE

You will join our real-time visualisation team of fourteen, reporting to
the Design Technology Lead. This is a senior individual contributor role —
you will own the shader and material pipeline end to end, and you will be
the person the art team comes to when something needs to be automated,
optimised or made possible.

Day to day you will:

· Own and evolve the shader and material pipeline in Unreal Engine
· Build and maintain the Python tooling our artists rely on daily
· Create procedural content systems in Houdini for environment and variant
  generation
· Work directly with engineering on frame budgets for embedded automotive
  hardware, where the constraints are real and unforgiving
· Maintain our USD-based interchange between modelling, lighting and
  engineering
· Contribute to functional safety documentation alongside our systems team

WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR

Essential:
· Senior-level Unreal Engine experience in a production environment
· Strong Python for pipeline and tooling work
· Houdini for procedural content generation
· Shader authoring in HLSL or an equivalent shading language
· Practical USD pipeline experience
· Maya
· Familiarity with ISO 26262 or comparable functional safety standards

Nice to have:
· Automotive or simulation background
· Experience optimising for embedded or mobile GPUs
· Swedish language, though our working language is English

We would also love someone who is:
· A genuine team player who lifts the people around them
· Comfortable with ambiguity and able to self-direct
· A clear communicator across disciplines

WHAT WE OFFER

· Competitive salary, reviewed annually
· 30 days holiday plus Swedish public holidays
· Occupational pension (ITP1) and full parental leave top-up
· Wellness allowance of 5,000 SEK per year
· Hardware of your choosing
· A four-day week in July

HOW TO APPLY

Send a CV and, if you have one, a reel or portfolio. We review on a rolling
basis and aim to respond within a week. We interview in two stages: a
conversation with the team, then a technical discussion built around your
own past work — we do not set take-home tests.

Volvia Interactive is an equal opportunity employer. We welcome applicants
regardless of background, and we are happy to make adjustments to our
process on request.
